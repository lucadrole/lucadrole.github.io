Presented research on novel computational methods for analyzing high-resolution ex vivo MRI data, with applications in neuroanatomical research and brain connectivity studies.

## Abstract
This presentation showcased the work conducted at the Laboratory for Ex vivo Modeling of Neuroanatomy (LEMoN) at the Martinos Center for Biomedical Imaging. The talk covered:

* Novel image registration techniques for high-resolution ex vivo MRI
* Deep learning approaches for automated neuroanatomical segmentation
* Validation methodologies and clinical applications
* Open-source tools for the research community

## Key Topics
- High-resolution ex vivo MRI processing challenges
- State-of-the-art registration and segmentation algorithms
- Machine learning applications in neuroimaging
- Collaborative research in computational neuroanatomy

*Presented as part of ongoing research collaboration with Harvard Medical School*
